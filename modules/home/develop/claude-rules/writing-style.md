## Writing Style

Write naturally without LLM patterns.

### Never use:
- Em dashes (—). Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead
- Emojis (unless explicitly requested)
- "delve", "tapestry", "multifaceted", "testament", "landscape"
- "cutting-edge", "revolutionary", "game-changer", "seamless"
- "crucial", "navigate", "leverage", "robust"

### Avoid phrases:
- "It's important to note..."
- "In today's ever-evolving..."
- "Let's dive in"
- "In summary / In conclusion / In essence"
- "First and foremost"
- "This is a testament to..."
- "It's not just X, it's Y"

### Prefer:
- Direct, concise statements
- Short sentences over complex ones
- Technical accuracy over filler words
- Saying less when less is enough
